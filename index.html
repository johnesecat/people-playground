Runs entirely in your browser. This Unity build needs WebGL 2 and substantial free memory; older iPhones may not be able to load it.

Made for touch

Drag
One finger drags: it picks up and moves objects, and clicks game menus. A quick tap is a click; a still long press opens the object menu.
Zoom
Put two fingers down together, in either order, then pinch to zoom.
Rotate
Keep the left finger still on an object, then move the right finger up (rotate left) or down (rotate right).
Activate / fire
While one finger drags an object, touch with another finger to hold F (activate) until that finger lifts.
Spawn & place
Pick the facing (← or → shows the current choice), tap Spawn, then Place to put the object at the last touched spot. Shift and Alt are latched in More.

Twisting emulates rotation keys, not a direct object-angle API. Match this speed to the game's rotation rate if it feels too fast or slow. Shortcuts assume the game's default bindings; custom bindings must be restored in game. No Unity project source is included.

On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen for a larger display. Fullscreen is not available on every iOS version. Use the game's Settings for volume and graphics.
