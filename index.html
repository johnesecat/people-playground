People Playground

A physics sandbox, now within reach.

About 164 MB to download. Landscape is recommended on phones.

Runs entirely in your browser. This Unity build needs WebGL 2 and substantial free memory; older iPhones may not be able to load it.

Made for touch

Grab
Drag with one finger to pick up objects, use tools, and click game menus. Double-tap an object to activate it.
Two-finger rotation
In Grab, keep your first finger on the object and twist a second finger around it. This drives the game's A / D rotation; use ↶ / ↷ for fine control.
Activate
Tap an object to send F without grabbing. Hold Use / fire to keep activating while your other finger aims or drags.
Camera
Drag to pan. Pinch with two fingers to zoom and move them together to pan. Grab reserves two fingers for rotation so a twist never accidentally zooms.
Inspect
Tap for the object's right-click menu, then return to Grab to choose an entry. In Grab, a stationary long-press also opens this menu. Freeze and other object actions live there.
Items & tools
Tap Items, choose the spawn facing (← or → is outlined when active), then Spawn at the last touched position. Latch Shift for faster rotation / multiselect, or Alt for snapping. More includes Copy, Paste, Details, Thermal, and tools 1–9. Tools / powers sends Left Ctrl.

Twisting emulates rotation keys, not a direct object-angle API. Match this speed to the game's rotation rate if it feels too fast or slow. Shortcuts assume the game's default bindings; custom bindings must be restored in game. No Unity project source is included.

On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen for a larger display. Fullscreen is not available on every iOS version. Use the game's Settings for volume and graphics.
